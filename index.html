How It Works

You're one minute away from your first sentence.

No account. No configuration. Add a book, double-tap, and you're reading. That's the whole setup.

Add a book

Tap the + button in your library and pick any EPUB from your Files app. TapLine processes it automatically — no fiddling required.

2

One double-tap hides everything except one sentence. No settings to adjust. You're already in focus mode.

Features

Every feature is designed to help you read more.

Not just read differently — actually read more. More books, more often, more easily than you could before.

🎯

Single-Sentence Mode

Hide the full page and navigate one sentence at a time with a tap or swipe. No visual clutter, no pressure from how far you still have to go.

❤️

Save Lines That Catch You

Like a sentence or add a comment without losing your place. Review all saved ideas across every book in one panel.

📊

Reading Stats That Motivate

Daily, weekly, and monthly charts show your reading time and progress. Watching the bars grow creates its own dopamine loop — for books.

Total Visual Comfort

📚

Smart EPUB Library
